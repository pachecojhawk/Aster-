About Aster's abilities

Aster is an original, small rule-based system—not a neural language model. Version 3.1 adds explicit question-type recognition, limited self-assessment, simple comparisons, and uncertainty reporting. These procedures do not establish understanding, consciousness, or a conscience. It recognizes some questions, remembers explicitly taught facts, and retrieves Wikipedia article summaries when asked about unfamiliar subjects. It does not truly understand arbitrary sentences or verify sources independently. Online information can be incomplete or wrong. Its autonomous research uses related-article links, not independent human-like curiosity or deep reasoning.

Automatic memories are saved only on this browser. Export backups regularly. Research requests go to Wikipedia; no OpenAI key or paid service is required.
